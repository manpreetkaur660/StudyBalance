ABOUT STUDYBALANCE

A simple planner for university life

StudyBalance is an academic web application prototype designed to support university students with task organisation, deadline awareness, focused study and study-life balance.

How your data is stored

Your tasks, progress and mood selection are saved in your browser using LocalStorage. No personal information is sent to a server. Clearing browser storage will remove the saved information.

Academic prototype: This is not a commercial application or a replacement for professional wellbeing support.
